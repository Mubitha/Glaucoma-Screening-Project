Glaucoma Resources

Here are useful resources related to glaucoma — including education, support, research, and patient-focused tools you can explore.

Global Awareness & Professional Organizations

A global organization working to eliminate glaucoma-related disability through education, World Glaucoma Week events, and access to scientific journals and webinars.

Tips for Using These Resources

Support groups (online or local) can help you connect with others going through the same journey, share coping strategies, and learn practical tips.

Educational materials and webinars help you understand your condition, treatment choices, and how to talk to your eye doctor.

Awareness campaigns like World Glaucoma Week help spread knowledge about early detection and prevention.
