AI-Based Early Childhood Glaucoma Risk Screening System

About Us

Early childhood glaucoma is a serious eye disorder that can cause permanent vision loss if not detected at an early stage. This AI-based system analyzes eye images to identify potential glaucoma risk in children and assists doctors in early diagnosis.

Why Early Detection is Important?

Childhood glaucoma can silently damage vision if not diagnosed early. Many children may not express symptoms clearly, making AI-assisted screening crucial for timely medical intervention.

Complete System Workflow

Step 1: User Registration

Parents or doctors securely register in the system.

Step 2: Patient Data Entry

Child’s medical and eye details are entered into the system.

Step 3: AI Risk Analysis

The AI model analyzes data and predicts glaucoma risk level.

Step 4: Dashboard Results

Users can view risk reports and recommended next steps.
