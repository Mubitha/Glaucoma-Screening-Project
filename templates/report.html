Image Processing Pipeline — 6-Stage Analysis

The fundus image passes through 6 automated preprocessing stages before CDR calculation. Each stage refines the image to isolate and measure the optic disc with precision.

Step 01
Image Captured
Raw webcam or uploaded frame — starting point for all processing.
Step 02
Grayscale
Converts to single channel. Removes colour noise, retains structure.
Step 03
Noise Removal
Gaussian blur smooths pixel noise for cleaner disc edges.
Step 04
Binary Threshold
Separates foreground disc structures from background tissue.
Step 05
Eye Region
Haar cascade isolates the eye ROI from surrounding facial area.
Step 06
Cup Detected
Largest contour = optic cup. CDR computed from cup/disc area ratio.
